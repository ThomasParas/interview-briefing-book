Story bank

Say · Story

"Tell me about a tricky issue you took to the root cause."

The time-traveling shipments

  1. Headline
    Shipments "delivered" before pickupOne carrier's shipments started showing as delivered before they'd been picked up.
  2. Setup
    Messages valid; billing on holdEvery message passed validation, nothing in our parser had changed, and billing had frozen 140 loads because the timeline made no sense.
  3. What I did
    Lined up raw timestamps → offset missingI lined up the raw messages against our parsed events and saw the carrier had dropped the time zone offset after a system upgrade. We were reading Pacific times as UTC.
  4. The choice
    Patched them, then guarded everyoneI patched that carrier's mapping the same day, then added a check that flags any carrier whose events arrive out of order, so it couldn't happen silently again.
  5. Result
    Billing unfrozen same day; check caught 2 moreBilling released the loads that afternoon, and the check caught two other carriers with the same problem within a quarter.
  6. Bridge
    What changed isn't always what brokeNothing on our side had changed. The seam had. That's the first question I'd ask on a broken integration here.

Follow-ups to expect: How did you spot the offset? (Lined up three raw messages in a spreadsheet, so the six-hour gap was obvious.) Why not ask the carrier to fix it? (Did both: patched our side to unblock billing, and sent their engineer the evidence.)

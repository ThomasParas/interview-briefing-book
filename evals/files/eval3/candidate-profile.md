## Stories
### Webhook replay tool
updated: 2026-09-20
- Situation: customers lost events when their endpoints were down, and the only fix was an engineer manually replaying them.
- What I did: built an internal tool (Python, small Flask UI) that let support replay failed webhooks for a customer and time window.
- Why: I was the one getting paged at 2am to replay events by hand, and it was always the same 20-minute procedure.
- Result: escalations to engineering for replays dropped about 30% in the first quarter; engineering later made it a customer-facing feature.
- Good for: automation, removing repeat work.
